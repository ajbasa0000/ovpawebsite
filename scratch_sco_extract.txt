1.0     Objectives             To ensure efficient and secure operation of the cash management process, contributing to the overall financial health and stability of the              organization.    2.0     Scope             The scope of this document includes the following procedure:   2.1. Collect cash from the stakeholder. 2.2. Issue an official receipt (O.R.) 2.3. Deposit the collection in the landbank. 2.4. MANDATE: UP System Cash Office (SCO) The UP-System Cash Office (SCO) was created at the 1144 th  meeting of the BOR on 31 August 2000.  In the past, the UP Diliman Cash Office used to service the UP-System Administration offices/units.  However, the increase in the number of offices/units at the System level also increased the volume of transactions being processed by the said office. In the performance of its functions, the SCO is guided by the rules and regulations set by the National Government Accounting System (NGAS).  It adheres strictly to the provision of the NGAS Manual which contains the procedural guidelines for all the official transaction of the office. Function and Services of the UP-System Cash Office Serve the UP-System Offices and the Constituent Universities (CUs) and units in the management, releases, reporting and maintenance of funds, under the New Government Accounting System (NGAS). Prepare regular cash management reports on the following: Cash Receipts; Cash Disbursement; and Investments for submission to the Commission of Audit through the OVPPF, the UP President the Department of Budget and Management (DBM); and  Issue receipt for payments of transactions of UP System offices (except, the UP Admissions Office).

DECISIONIS OF THE BOARD OF REGENTS 1144 TH  MEETING, 31 AUGUST 2000 MATTER ARISING FROM THE MINUTES OF THE 1143 RD  MEETING 29 JUNE 2000 Creation of a UP System Cash Office Justification: At present the Cash office of the UP Diliman also services the UP System. In recent years, the number of UP System Administration (UPSA) offices/units and the volume of transactions have increased due to the increase in the number of Constituent Universities and due to the necessity of increasing the Number of units at the system level. This requires the creation of a Separate Cash Office for the UP System. The UP Diliman Cash Office must be relieved of the task of servicing these UPSA units.  The UPS Cashier must be given the time to prepare “System” reports required by the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) and the UP System Administration. DBM requires prompt and accurate submission of reports. Reports of UP System Administration include cash position reports, cash receipts reports, cash disbursement reports and investment reports. Supervision:  The proposed UP System Cash Office shall be under the supervision of the Office of the Vice President and investment reports. Staff:  The Vice President for Administration shall determine the staffing pattern of the UP System Cash Office. Initially, personnel from other UPSA units will be assigned to UP System Cash Office and will be paid honoraria, until such time when personnel positions shall have been created. UP System Cash Office started on May 5, 2001
