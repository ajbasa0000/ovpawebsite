# OVPA Website & Content Management System (CMS) User Guide

An intuitive, executive-friendly guide to the **Office of the Vice President for Administration (OVPA) Website** for university leaders, administrative staff, content managers, and general stakeholders.

---

## 🏛️ What is the OVPA Website?

The OVPA Website is the central digital hub for the **Office of the Vice President for Administration** at the University of the Philippines System. It serves as a single, accessible platform for:
* **Public Transparency & Governance**: Communicating university mandates, institutional policies, quality management systems, and strategic goals.
* **Staff Directory & Organizational Structure**: Introducing executive leadership, administrative personnel, and specialized units like the OVPA-Quality Management System (OVPA-QMS).
* **Services & Resources Hub**: Providing easy access to administrative services, official memos (issuances), downloadable forms, and university announcements.
* **Content Management System (CMS)**: Enabling staff to update website content, publish news, post events, and review constituent inquiries without technical coding.

---

## 🌐 Website Features & Main Sections

### 1. Home Page (`/`)
* **Welcome Banner & Institutional Identity**: Highlighting the office's core motto *"Honor and Excellence in University Administration"*.
* **Featured News Carousel**: Showcasing major announcements, administrative milestones, and policy updates.
* **Upcoming Events & Latest Issuances**: Quick access to university activities, workshops, and recently published memorandums.

---

### 2. About OVPA (`/about/`)
* **Institutional Pillars**: Details the official **Vision**, **Mission**, **Mandate**, and **Core Values** (Transparency, Integrity, Excellence, Accountability).
* **Historical Overview**: Trace the history of the office since its creation by the Board of Regents in 1969.
* **Key Functions & Strategic 10-Point Plan**: Outlines HR management, financial authorization, digital transformation, and operational efficiency goals.
* **🧭 Easy Side Navigation**: Features a sticky **"On This Page"** side tab bar that lets readers jump directly to specific topics (such as *Historical Overview* or *Strategic Goals*) as they scroll.

---

### 3. Quality Policy & Kalinangán Values (`/page/quality-policy/`)
* **UPSA Quality Policy**: Official statement signed by UP President Angelo A. Jimenez committing to ISO 9001 quality management standards.
* **The 7 Operational Drivers**: Colored card breakdown of the office's core drivers (Stakeholder Engagement, Exceeding Expectations, Regulatory Compliance, Knowledge & Innovation, Enabling Environment, Resource Optimization, and UN Sustainable Development Goals).
* **Kalinangán Code of Conduct**: Practical guidelines for staff embodying **Honor (Dangal)** and **Excellence (Husay)** in daily operations.
* **Official Signed Artifact Gallery**: Expandable gallery links to high-resolution official posters signed by university leadership.

---

### 4. Office Structure & Staff Directory (`/office/`)
Organized into clear visual tiers:
1. **Executive Leadership**: Highlights top management (Vice President and Assistant Vice Presidents).
2. **Administrative Personnel**: Cards displaying staff members, their titles, and support roles.
3. **OVPA - Quality Management System (QMS)**: Dedicated team section highlighting personnel responsible for quality assurance and process improvement.

---

### 5. Resources & Services Hub (`/resources/`)
* **Administrative Services Catalog** (`/resources/services/`): Searchable catalog detailing services, expected processing times, requirements, and step-by-step procedures.

### 6. Public Desk & Feedback (`/contact/` & `/feedback/`)

## ✏️ How Staff Can Update Content (The CMS)

No coding skills are required to update website information. Authorized staff members have access to a simple dashboard:

```
[Staff Login] ➔ [Select Content Category] ➔ [Fill Out Form / Rich Text] ➔ [Click Publish]
```

### 1. Staff Access Portal
Staff members can log in by clicking the **"Staff Access Portal"** link in the website footer or navigating to:
* **Staff Dashboard**: [http://localhost:8000/dashboard/](http://localhost:8000/dashboard/)
* **Master Admin Console**: [http://localhost:8000/admin/](http://localhost:8000/admin/)

### 2. Common Content Updates

| Action | How to Do It |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Publishing a News Article** | Go to Dashboard ➔ **Add News Article** ➔ Enter Title, Brief Summary, Main Story Text, Upload Featured Photo, and check **Featured** if it should appear on the homepage. |
| **Posting an Event** | Go to Dashboard ➔ **Add Event** ➔ Enter Event Name, Location, Event Type (Meeting, Workshop, Seminar), and Start/End Date & Time. |
| **Updating Staff Directory** | Go to Admin Console ➔ **Staff Members** ➔ Click on a staff name to update their position title, unit, or display sequence. |
| **Adding Downloadable Documents** | Go to Admin Console ➔ **Documents** ➔ Upload the file, enter a descriptive title, choose a category (e.g. *Forms* or *Policies*), and save. |
| **Reviewing Inquiries & Feedback** | Go to Dashboard ➔ **Contact Inquiries** or **Feedback** to read incoming submissions and mark them as resolved or reviewed. |
