Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries regarding administrative processes, university policies, and office services.

How do I request an official issuance copy?

You can search for and download official issuances directly from our Issuances Portal. If you need a certified true copy, please submit a formal request through our Contact page.

Where can I find the forms for HR processing?

All downloadable forms, including those for HR, Accounting, and Supply Management, are available in the Document Repository under the 'Forms' category.

What is the typical processing time for a Notice of Award?

Based on our administrative services charter, a standard Notice of Award processing takes approximately 20 minutes once all required documents are verified by the SSPMO. For more details, view our Services Catalog.
